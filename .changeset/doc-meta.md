`DocMeta` in `@ekdotico/domico/components`, with `styles/doc-meta.css`: a document's version and last-updated date on one line ("Version 1.2 · Last updated 2026-09-30"). The application supplies both values. A missing value, or a date that is not YYYY-MM-DD, is shown as "unset" or "unknown" in a bordered strip instead of being hidden. Below 360px the words "Last updated" leave the visual line and stay in the accessible name.
