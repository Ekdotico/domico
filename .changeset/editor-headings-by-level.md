The editor stylesheet styles a heading by `data-level="1"`, `"2"` or `"3"` when the editor sets one, on any heading tag, so an application can draw the text's headings as `h2`, `h3` and `h4` and keep their look. The tag rules stay: an `h1`, `h2` or `h3` with no level looks as it did. Nothing a reader sees changes.
