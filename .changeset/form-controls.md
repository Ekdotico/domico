'@ekdotico/domico': minor

Form controls, a dialog and a tooltip: the controls the Ekdotico application builds its sign-in pages, account settings, feedback form and confirmations from. `@ekdotico/domico/components` now exports `Button`, `TextField`, `PasswordField`, `OneTimeCodeField`, `TextArea`, `RadioGroup`, `Checkbox`, `FormFault`, `Dialog` and `Tooltip`, with the types `ButtonVariant`, `TextFieldProps`, `RadioOption` and `FormFaultItem`. Four stylesheet entries carry them: `styles/button.css`, `styles/form.css` (the fields, the choices and the form fault together), `styles/dialog.css` and `styles/tooltip.css`. All four are also in `styles.css` and `styles/components.css`. The new classes are prefixed `ekd-`, so nothing an application already styles is renamed or restyled.

Each control is the platform's own element drawn from tokens, and takes its words from the application; none has a default label.

- `Button` has four variants (`primary`, `secondary`, `quiet`, `destructive`), defaults to `type="button"`, and carries the pending state `GateSubmitButton` had: `aria-disabled` with a click guard, so focus stays on the button while the action is in flight. `GateSubmitButton` is now this component with the gate's class and its two default labels. Its props, its markup and its stylesheet are unchanged.
- `TextField`, `PasswordField`, `OneTimeCodeField` and `TextArea` share a visible label, an optional hint and a message. `error` marks the control `aria-invalid`, and the control is described by its hint and message through `aria-describedby`. `PasswordField` has a show-and-hide button with `aria-pressed` and one name in both states, requires `autoComplete`, and hides the password again when its form is submitted. `OneTimeCodeField` is one input with `inputMode="numeric"` and `autoComplete="one-time-code"`, six digits by default. `TextArea` counts against `maximum` in words the application supplies, and announces the count politely a second after typing stops. None of the four takes a placeholder.
- `RadioGroup` is a fieldset named by its legend with native radios and none preselected. `Checkbox` is a native checkbox inside its label.
- `FormFault` is the statement of a fault at the head of a form: `role="alert"`, focused when it appears and whenever `focusKey` changes, with links that move focus into the fields at fault.
- `Dialog` is the native `<dialog>` opened with `showModal()`, named by its heading. The application holds `open`; Escape and the close button call `onClose`. Focus returns to the control that opened it.
- `Tooltip` is shown on hover and on keyboard focus, tied to its trigger by `aria-describedby`, and meets WCAG 2.2 success criterion 1.4.13: Escape dismisses it without moving the pointer or focus, the pointer can move onto it, and nothing times it out. It opens under or over the start edge of its trigger and does not reposition at the edge of the viewport.

No token is added. A dialog and a tooltip sit on `--bg-raised`, where `--fg-3` (4.40:1, sepia dark), `--accent` (4.42:1, parchment dark) and `--danger` (4.27:1, parchment dark) do not hold as text, so these controls set text in `--fg-1` and `--fg-2` and use `--accent` and `--danger` as boundaries and marks. `check:contrast` gains seven pairs for it, and `check:components` gains eight cases.

For a consumer: `ekdotico-check discipline` derives its list of reimplemented components from this barrel, so from this version a local component exported as `Button`, `Dialog`, `Tooltip`, `Checkbox`, `TextField`, `TextArea`, `RadioGroup`, `PasswordField`, `OneTimeCodeField` or `FormFault` is reported. The documentation site, deree and praktico export none of those today.
